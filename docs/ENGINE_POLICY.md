# Engine policy

Version 1 uses a 3.5% margin, 1–10 ticks, a 30-second settlement-delay refund threshold, ten-second feed lag threshold, 1% minimum profit ratio, and 30-day revealed-tick retention. DEMO limits are USD 1.00–1,000.00 stake, 20 open contracts, 30 purchases per minute, and USD 100,000 per-tick liability. Cent-floor rounding can make effective margin slightly higher for small stakes and favors the house.
