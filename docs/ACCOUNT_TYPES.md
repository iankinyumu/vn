# Account types

Practice and future Real accounts share engine algorithms and policy economics. They never share a wallet, ledger, exposure bucket, index row, epoch, seed, tick, or digit stream. The parity gate scans new `*_engine_*.sql` migrations for behavior branches by mode; mode is otherwise carried as a typed key.

Before real money: seed custody outside the database, Real creation, funding and currency conversion, Daraja callbacks and idempotent receipts, reconciliation, load testing, step-up sign-in, cashier flows, and independent engine audit are deliberately absent.
