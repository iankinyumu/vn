The house margin and the payout formula

Every payout comes from one formula and one published number, the house margin. This guide shows the formula, works through examples and explains the rounding.

The formula

payout = floor( stake × (1 − margin) × 10 ÷ winning digits ), to the cent
profit = payout − stake

The winning digits are the digits that win for the contract type: 5 for Even or Odd, 1 for Match, 9 for Differ, and so on (see the settlement guide). The payout includes the stake. If the contract loses, nothing is paid and the stake is lost.

The calculation is done on the server in exact decimal arithmetic, never in floating point, and the quote on the trade page shows the result before you buy: payout, profit, win probability and the policy version used.

The margin: published and configurable

Policy version 1, the version the platform ships with, sets a house margin of 3.5% for every contract type. The margin is configurable: staff can publish a new policy version with a margin between 0.5% and 15%, and a version may give a contract type its own margin within the same bounds. Version 1 has no per-type margins.

A new version applies to new purchases only. Every contract records the policy version, payout and multiplier it was bought with, and a later policy never changes a contract you already hold. The quote always uses the version in force at that moment.

What the margin means

Without a margin, a fair payout would return exactly the stake on average: stake × 10 ÷ winning digits. The margin takes a fixed share of that. At 3.5%, an Even contract pays 1.93 times the stake. Every contract type is priced with the same margin, so no type is cheaper than another. Past results do not change the odds of the next one.

Rounding always goes down

The payout is floored to the cent, never rounded up. On most stakes this changes nothing, but on some small stakes the effective margin is slightly above the published one. For example, a 1.01 Even stake has an exact value of 1.9493 and pays 1.94. The payout shown before you buy is always the exact amount paid.

Offers the engine refuses

The engine will not sell a contract whose profit would be less than 1% of the stake (the minimum profit ratio in policy version 1). Before a policy version is published, the server checks that every enabled contract type, at every allowed barrier, clears this minimum at the smallest allowed stake.

Stakes and limits

Accounts are denominated in USD. Under policy version 1, a stake is between 1.00 and 1,000.00, an account may hold at most 20 open contracts and buy at most 30 per minute, and a single settle tick carries a capped total liability. The server checks each limit on every purchase.
