Cookie Policy

Version 2026-10-03. Last updated 3 October 2026.

In short: SmartProfit sets no advertising or analytics cookies. It keeps your sign-in session and a few security records on your device because the site cannot work without them. Settings you choose, such as appearance, are remembered only if you allow it.

Storage on this device

What we store and why

SmartProfit sets one first-party cookie, sp_consent, to remember your answer to this page. Everything else lives in your browser's local storage and session storage, which the law treats the same way as cookies, so this policy covers both. Session storage is cleared when you close the tab; local storage stays until it is removed.

Third parties

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr. These services receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads, as any website you visit does, but they set no cookies for SmartProfit and we receive no tracking data from them. See the Privacy Policy for every provider we use.

Changing your mind

Use the form above, or in the footer of any page. Withdrawing consent removes the preference storage straight away. You can also clear all site data in your browser's settings; that signs you out.
