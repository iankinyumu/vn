About SmartProfit

A practice workspace for digit contracts, built so that every settled outcome can be re-checked afterwards.

From an index to a digit

SmartProfit runs five self-generated indices, SPI10 to SPI100. Each one ticks on a fixed cadence and publishes a price with three decimal places.

A contract settles on the final digit of the tick that closes it. New ticks come from a seeded price generator: the price is published first, and its final decimal digit is the outcome. Each possible final digit remains equally likely.

Practice now, real later

A practice account carries virtual USD credits and runs the identical engine path a funded account would use: the same quote, the same purchase, the same settlement and the same ledger.

Practice credits have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn and are not a forecast of any funded result. A funded account type is designed for but deliberately not built yet.

What to expect

  • Only practice trading is available. There is no deposit, withdrawal, payment or identity-verification path in the product.
  • Every quote is computed on the server from the published policy version. The browser never sends a price, a payout or an outcome.
  • Payouts are floored to the cent and a small stake can round slightly above the nominal margin. That rounding always favours the house.
  • Accounts can carry restrictions that limit stake size, open contracts or daily losses. When one applies, the reason and any expiry are shown on the page.
  • Commit–reveal proves a seed was not swapped after it was committed. It does not prove the operator picked that seed in good faith, and it does not make outcomes predictable.
