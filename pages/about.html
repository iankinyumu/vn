Our Story

2022
The Beginning

SmartProfitBinary was founded by a team of fintech veterans and blockchain enthusiasts with a vision to create the most user-friendly cryptocurrency exchange. Launching with just 50 coins and a handful of employees.

Early 2023
Rapid Growth

Reached 100,000 users within 6 months of launch. Introduced advanced trading features including futures trading, margin trading, and staking rewards. Expanded to 150+ coins.

Mid 2023
Institutional Grade

Launched institutional services with API access, OTC trading desk, and custody solutions. Secured $50M in Series A funding from top venture capital firms.
