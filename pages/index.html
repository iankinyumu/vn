Independent draws, published economics.

Two rules carry the product: outcomes are independent, and everything that decides money is visible before the contract is bought.

Price decides the digit

Each new tick has a generated price, and its last decimal digit settles the contract. Every final digit is equally likely; past results do not predict the next one.

The house margin is public

Payouts are computed from the published margin, 3.5% in policy version 1, and the number of winning digits. Payouts are floored to the cent, so rounding always favours the house, never you.

Commitments are chained

Each epoch publishes its seed commitment before use and chains it to the previous one, so a changed seed is detectable after the fact.
