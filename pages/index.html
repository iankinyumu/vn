Built around the essentials

Independent draws, published economics.

Two rules carry the product: outcomes are independent, and everything that decides money is visible before the contract is bought.

Each digit is drawn independently

A tick's digit comes from a keyed stream over the tick number. The price path is cosmetic: it never decides an outcome, and neither does the last result.

The house margin is public

Payouts are computed from a published margin and the number of winning digits. Uplift is floored to the cent, so the rounding always favours the house, never you.

Commitments are chained

Each epoch publishes its seed commitment before use and chains it to the previous one, so a changed seed is detectable after the fact.
