Risk Disclosure

Version 2026-10-03. Last updated 3 October 2026.

Read this before you trade. A digit contract either pays out or loses the whole stake. Payouts are set below fair odds, so over many contracts the expected result is a loss. No strategy, pattern or streak changes that.

How you can lose

  • Each contract can lose its full stake. There is no partial refund when a contract loses.
  • The odds favour SmartProfit. Every payout is below what the true probability would pay, and it is rounded down to the cent. That margin is built in on every contract.
  • Outcomes are random. Each final digit from 0 to 9 is equally likely on every tick. Past digits, streaks and charts do not predict the next one, and no system can beat that.
  • Losses add up fast. Contracts settle in seconds, so many can be placed in a short time.

Practice is not a forecast

Practice accounts use virtual credits with no cash value. Results in Practice do not show what you would win or lose with money, and a good run in Practice is not a reason to trade for real.

Real mode

Real mode is in testing and is not open to the public. Where it is switched on, its credits are test-only, but an M-Pesa prompt sent to your own phone charges real money. Only stake money you can afford to lose entirely, and never borrowed money or money meant for bills, rent or family needs.

Staying in control

  • Set a take-profit and a stop-loss for each session on the trading page, and stop when either is reached.
  • Take a break when the trading companion suggests one, and do not try to win back losses.
  • Ask us through the Contact page to set stricter limits on your account or to close it.
  • If trading stops being something you choose freely, or affects your money, sleep, work or relationships, stop and talk to someone you trust or a professional counsellor.

Checking results

You can re-check settled ticks on the Fairness page. Verification proves a published seed was not swapped after it was committed. It does not make outcomes predictable and does not change the odds above.
