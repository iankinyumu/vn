Risk Disclosure

Version 2026-10-03. Last updated 3 October 2026.

Read this before you trade. A digit contract either pays out or loses its stake. Only trade money you can afford to lose.

How you can lose

  • Each contract can lose its full stake. There is no partial refund when a contract loses.
  • Payouts include a margin. Each payout is set from the published margin, and the exact amount is shown before you buy.
  • Outcomes are random. Each final digit from 0 to 9 is equally likely on every tick. Past digits and charts do not predict the next one.
  • Contracts are fast. They settle in seconds, so set a stake and session limit that suits you.

Practice and Real

Practice uses virtual credit, so results there do not involve your money. Real uses your own money, with the same contracts and pricing.

Money you trade with

Only stake money you can afford to lose, and never borrowed money or money meant for essential costs.

Staying in control

  • Set a take-profit and a stop-loss for each session on the trading page, and stop when either is reached.
  • Take a break when the trading companion suggests one, and do not try to win back losses.
  • Ask us through the Contact page to set stricter limits on your account or to close it.
  • If trading stops being something you choose freely, or affects your money, sleep, work or relationships, stop and talk to someone you trust or a professional counsellor.

Checking results

You can re-check settled ticks on the Fairness page. Verification proves a published seed was not swapped after it was committed. It does not make outcomes predictable and does not change the odds above.
