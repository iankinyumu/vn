How this contract works

Pick Even or Odd. The last digit of the settle tick decides: 0, 2, 4, 6 and 8 are even; 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9 are odd.

Pick a digit. Matches wins only if the settle tick ends in it; Differs wins on any other digit, so it pays less.

Pick a digit. Over wins if the settle digit is higher, Under if it is lower. A settle digit equal to yours loses both.

Payout is the total paid back if the contract wins, your stake included. If it loses, the stake is lost. How payouts are worked out
