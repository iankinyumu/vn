6. Digit contracts, quotes and settlement

  • A digit contract settles on the last decimal digit of a published index tick. Each digit from 0 to 9 is equally likely, and earlier digits do not change the odds of later ones.
  • Every quote, payout and result is computed by our servers from the published policy. A purchase is final once confirmed and cannot be cancelled.
  • Payouts are set below fair odds and are rounded down to the cent. This margin is how the service is paid for, so over many contracts the expected result for a customer is a loss.
  • A contract is settled by the tick it names. If an index pauses or a tick cannot be published, open contracts are handled as described on the trading page and in the guides.
