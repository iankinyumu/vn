Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-03. Last updated 3 October 2026.

In short: we collect what we need to run your account, settle contracts, keep the service secure and answer support requests. We do not sell personal data and we use no advertising or analytics trackers.

This policy is written to meet the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. SmartProfit is the data controller for the personal data described here.

1. What we collect

You give us

  • Account details: first and last name, username, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash by our authentication provider; we never see them.
  • Welcome answers (optional, asked once after sign-up and changeable on your profile): why you are here, how familiar you are with trading, which contract types interest you and where you wanted to start. We use them to tailor the site, for example which contract the trade page opens on and whether the first-visit tour runs.
  • Profile choices: the character you pick as a profile picture and your notification settings.
  • Support requests: the name, contact email, topic and messages you send, and our replies.
  • Real mode payments (testing only, on accounts where it is switched on): the M-Pesa phone number you enter and the payment references and statuses returned by Safaricom.

Created when you use the service

  • Trading records: quotes, contracts, stakes, payouts, settlements and the ledger entries for your Practice and Real balances.
  • Account controls: limits, restrictions and the reasons for them.
  • Welcome answers: while your account is open.
  • Notifications: messages sent to you and whether you have read them.
  • Security and technical data: sign-in times, IP address, browser and device details logged by our hosting and authentication providers, and a record of which version of these documents you accepted at sign-up.

We do not ask for identity documents or ID numbers, and we do not collect special categories of personal data.

3. Who processes it for us

These providers process personal data on our behalf, only for the purposes above:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and real-time updates. Holds your account, trading and support records.
  • Vercel: hosts the website. Receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page.
  • Resend: sends account emails. Receives your email address and the email content.
  • Safaricom (M-Pesa Daraja): processes Real mode payments. Receives the phone number and amount.
  • Content delivery networks (Google Fonts, cdnjs by Cloudflare, jsDelivr): serve fonts and code libraries, so they receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads. They set no cookies for us.

Our support staff see the account and support data they need to help you, and every staff action on an account is logged. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

4. Where it is stored

Some providers above store or process data outside Kenya. Where that happens we rely on the safeguards the Data Protection Act requires, such as the provider's contractual data protection terms, and we share only what each provider needs.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and trading records: while your account is open, then for as long as financial and audit record-keeping law requires.
  • Notifications: read notifications are deleted after 90 days and all notifications after 180 days.
  • Support requests: while your account is open, then for as long as needed to deal with any related complaint.
  • Data on your device: as set out in the Cookie Policy.

6. How we protect it

Data travels over encrypted connections. The database only lets each customer read their own records, and every request is checked on the server. Staff access needs a separate staff sign-in with two-factor authentication, and staff actions are logged. Sign-in, sign-up and email requests are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the Data Commissioner as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used (this policy);
  • get a copy of your personal data;
  • have inaccurate data corrected (you can change your name and profile on the profile page);
  • have data deleted, where we are not required to keep it;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, or restrict it;
  • have data you gave us sent to you or another service in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example through .

To use these rights, write to us through the Contact page (sign in first; if you cannot sign in, use "Can't sign in?" on the sign-in page). We reply within the time the law allows. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

8. Children

SmartProfit is only for people aged 18 or older. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we close it and delete the data we are not required to keep.

9. Changes and contact

When we change this policy, the version and date at the top change, and we tell signed-in customers about significant changes before they take effect. Questions go through the Contact page.
