About SmartProfitBinary

Get familiar with cryptocurrency markets and practise placing trades with virtual funds.

Follow the market

View price charts, explore trading pairs, and follow recent market activity. Use these tools to understand how prices change before placing a practice trade.

Market data can be delayed or temporarily unavailable. Check the connection status when using the trading page.

Practise with virtual funds

Your demo account lets you try buying and selling without spending real money. You can review your orders, balances, and holdings as you practise.

Demo funds have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. Practice trades do not buy or sell real cryptocurrency.

What to expect

  • Trading is currently available in demo mode. Real-money trading is not available.
  • Practice trades use market quotes to simulate a trade. The price you receive can differ from the price on the chart.
  • Demo results do not predict real returns. Real trading involves costs, changing prices, and the risk of losing money.

Open the trading page to explore the markets, or visit your dashboard to review your account.
